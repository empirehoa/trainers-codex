**Body:**

I've spent the last year building **Trainer's Codex** — a free web app for
building a team of six and immediately seeing what it's weak to, what it can't
hit, and what would wall it. No account, no install required (it's a PWA you
*can* install), works offline once loaded.

Things you can do in it right now:

- Pick any of the 1,307 species/forms (megas, regionals, Gmax, Paradox…),
  give them moves, items, shinies, nicknames and Tera types, and watch the
  coverage bar update live.
- **Journey Mode** — a run-based career sim. Every run is a seed, so
  `/journey?seed=8843` is the same run for everyone; there's a **daily** with
  a shared seed and a streak. At the end you get a Legend Card you can save or
  share. Nothing you can buy changes a score.
- Team posters in a dozen art styles (CRT, pixel grid, film…), sized for
  Instagram.
- Reference pages for every species and type
  (`trainerscodex.com/pokemon/gengar`) — weaknesses, stats, learnset,
  counters — plain fast pages that load nothing.
- Paste a Showdown team to import it; export back out.

It's free. There's an optional $4.99/mo (or $39/yr) premium for cosmetic stuff
— extra poster styles, Journey archive/finishes, more save slots. The analysis,
the daily and the leaderboard are and will stay free and unaffected.

https://trainerscodex.com

Independent fan project — not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak or The
Pokémon Company. Data via PokeAPI.

Feedback I'd most like: which region/story beats you want in Journey Mode, and
anything that's confusing on a phone.
