## Trainer's Codex v1.1.0-beta.1 — the new-games build

Beta: the code has passed the full test suite (lint 0 errors, 521 unit tests, 292 browser tests, 79 worker tests) and is ready for the live site; this tag marks the build that goes out.

### Every Mega, through Legends: Z-A and Pokémon Champions
- **26 new Megas** (Zygarde, Raichu X/Y, Lucario Z, Garchomp Z, Absol Z, Baxcalibur, Golisopod, Drampa, Falinks, Staraptor, Chimecho, Golurk, Meowstic, Crabominable, Scovillain, Glimmora, Heatran, Darkrai, Magearna, Zeraora, Tatsugiri and more) — **1,333 entries, 97 Megas**.
- Abilities follow the published Champions lists. The nine Megas with no published ability ship without one rather than a guess.
- **Megas now count as usable in Legends: Z-A** as well as Champions. A Mega team is routed to Z-A with a Mega Stone note instead of being told it has no mainline home.

### Pokémon Champions Regulation M-C
- New format preset **"Champions · Reg. M-C"**: the 346 species, forms and Megas usable in Ranked (Sep 9 – Dec 2, 2026). Illegal picks grey out, the team bar explains why, and the panel says when the regulation ends.

### Journey Mode: Mega Evolution
- One **Key Stone** per career, earned at the fourth badge. Spend it on your ace: Mega types in every matchup, the Mega sprite on your party, Legend Card and clip, and a small win-rate edge for the rest of the run. Old seeds replay exactly as before.

### Fixes
- Every Gigantamax form and 23 Champions Megas had **no moves** in the builder, matchup preview and reference pages. They now learn what their base species learns.
- A two-Pokémon team no longer reports "holds the most (1/6)"; the game matrix counts the real list instead of a fixed six.
- Learnsets are delta-encoded: the single-file bundle shrank from 2,138,956 to 1,989,092 bytes.

### For the release
- Deploy from a `main` checkout with `scripts/release.sh --deploy --verify` (PR #5 made the script accept `main`).
- Data refresh for Winds & Waves is one command: `node scripts/fetch-forms.mjs`.
